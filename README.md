# EduPath AI: Personalized Learning Curve Navigator

## Your idea in a nutshell
EduPath AI is a recommendation engine that dynamically adjusts educational content and study schedules based on a student's individual learning speed, focus intervals, and quiz performance, preventing burnout and optimization retention.

## Background
* **The Problem:** Standardized educational curricula follow a "one-size-fits-all" approach. Students who learn differently either get left behind or become bored, leading to high dropout rates in online and traditional courses.
* **Frequency:** This challenge affects millions of students engaging in remote learning and professional upskilling.
* **Motivation:** I want to democratize access to quality, adaptive mentorship, making complex fields like programming or mathematics accessible to anyone at their own natural pace.

## Data and AI Techniques
* **Data Sources:** 
  * Anonymized clickstream data from open-source learning management systems (LMS).
  * Student performance metrics (time spent per chapter, quiz scores, review intervals).
* **AI Techniques:**
  * **Logistic Regression / Classification Trees** to predict the probability of a student passing a specific module.
  * **Collaborative Filtering (Nearest Neighbor)** to recommend supplementary reading materials based on what helped similar students succeed.

## How is it used
The tool is integrated into online learning platforms or used as a standalone web dashboard by independent students and digital educators. It provides students with a tailored day-by-day roadmap, adjusting difficulty levels dynamically as they complete exercises.

## Challenges
* **Limitations:** EduPath AI cannot account for external human factors, such as sudden lack of internet access, personal emergencies, or lack of intrinsic mental motivation that prevents a student from logging in.

## What next
Future iterations could include Natural Language Processing (NLP) chatbots acting as 24/7 automated teaching assistants capable of explaining complex terms using analogies suited to the specific user's background.
